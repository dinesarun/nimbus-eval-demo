# Nimbus CRM — Refund & Billing Policy (internal help-center source)

## Refund windows
- Monthly plans: full refund if requested within 30 days of the purchase date.
- Annual plans: full refund if requested within 30 days of the purchase date.
- After 30 days, no refunds are available on any self-serve plan (monthly or annual). No partial or prorated refunds.
- Day 30 counts as inside the window. Day 31 onwards is outside the window.

## Enterprise customers
- Enterprise contracts are not refundable through self-serve.
- Enterprise customers must contact their dedicated account manager, or email enterprise@nimbuscrm.example.

## Add-ons (SMS credits, email credits)
- Unused add-on credits are refundable within 30 days of purchase.
- Used credits are never refundable.

## How to request a refund
- Go to Settings → Billing → Request Refund.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method only, within 5–10 business days.
- We cannot refund to a different card, bank account, gift card, or cryptocurrency.

## Account access problems
- If an account is locked (for example after 5 failed login attempts), the customer cannot use Settings.
- Locked customers must contact support at support@nimbuscrm.example to verify identity and unlock the account. Support can then process an eligible refund.

## Support
- Support hours: Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm IST.
- Support agents cannot approve exceptions to this policy.
